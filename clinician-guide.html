Why We Built This Free NHS Helper

In England, 1 in 6 hospital stays happen because medicines clash or cause side effects—and nearly half could be prevented. Sometimes a tablet finished two weeks ago is still active inside your liver today even when a blood test looks clear. Other times, practical needs—like an untreated hearing loss or a cold, damp home—are missed before clinical tests start.

OpenCareQ connects four calm helpers behind the scenes: a Trusted Rulebook Checker (never guesses or makes facts up), a Body Memory & Care-Order Helper (tracks 28-day tablet washouts and checks hearing/home support first), a Fair Clinic Organiser (protects winter slots for communities who wait longest), and a Split-Second Safety Brake (caught all 24 medicine traps in 48 safety tests).

Five Patient & Family Privacy Promises

  1. Your doctor, pharmacist, or midwife always decides with you—never a computer on its own.
  2. Zero personal details leave the NHS (zero names, zero NHS numbers, zero home addresses or dates of birth).
  3. Protected for public benefit via a non-profit UK Community Interest Company (INALYN CIC #17398798)—never to sell patient data.
  4. Normal NHS computers do 95% of checks in a split second at zero extra cloud cost.
  5. Shaped with patient, carer, deaf/hard-of-hearing, and maternity partners across Mid & South Essex before any clinic trial begins.
