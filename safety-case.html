NHS DCB0129 & DCB0160 Clinical Safety Case Suite (CSC-QT Audited)

To enable Clinical Safety Officers (CSOs), NHS ICB procurement/assurance teams, and peer reviewers to judge clinical safety quality from the statutory safety documents alone, OpenCareQ v0.8.0 publishes its complete 3-document safety suite—Clinical Safety Case Report (CSCR), Clinical Risk Management Plan (CRMP), and Cause-Separated Bow-Tie Hazard Log + Mid & South Essex (E54000026) DCB0160 Handover Pack—structured and evaluated across all 36 indicators of The Clinical Safety Case Quality Tool (CSC-QT).

Clinical Safety Case Report (CSCR) — OpenCareQ v0.8.0 (NHS DCB0129)

1. System & Clinical Scope (1.3–1.6): OpenCareQ v1.3.0 is a clean-room Non-Commercial NHS Public-Good (PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0 / CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) Python SDK, CLI, Marimo Workbench, and static audit portal for aggregate NHS ICB/PCN capacity stress-testing (42 English ICBs, 1,280 PCNs, 62.93M registered patients, phi_present = False), counterfactual coupled CYP3A4/PXR, CKM+PGx, and NHS PROGRESS + NICE TA1026 prevention modelling, and 1–10 year KDIGO ESKD / NICE £20,000/QALY DCEA. Explicit Exclusions: Direct point-of-care autonomous patient diagnosis or automated EHR prescription signing; acute physiological emergency triage (SpO2 < 92%, NEWS2 ≥ 5); paediatric, pregnancy, or Child-Pugh B/C hepatic failure dosing; and raw patient-identifiable data outside an IG-approved NHS SDE.

2. Structured Elicitation (2.1–2.2): Hazards were identified via 5-Layer Clinical Pathway FMEA, Relational Bow-Tie Barrier Mapping (strictly separating 16 contributing causes from 7 clinical hazards, resolving v0.5.0 conflations in H-OCQ-02 and H-OCQ-04), and 5 ML-DSA-65 adversarial negative-control policy traps (G1–G8), integrating Clinical Pharmacy, Urgent Primary Care, Social Prescribing WMTY, ICB Health Economics, and Safety/Quantum Engineering viewpoints.

3. Safety Conclusion & Undesirable (Score = 8) Risk/Benefit Justification (5.1–5.4): Layered controls eliminate all 3 initial Unacceptable (15–16) scores, leaving 0 Unacceptable, 2 Undesirable (Score = 8: H-OCQ-02, H-OCQ-03), 3 Tolerable (4–6), and 2 Acceptable (3). Both residual Undesirable (4×2 = 8) ratings reflect invariant Major (4) clinical harm severity (stroke, major bleed, AKI/ESKD) where final biological NICE DG59 genotyping (TC-OCQ-02) and BNF/SmPC/KDIGO verification (TC-OCQ-03) reside with human NHS clinicians. Against status-quo non-deployment—which leaves 13,490.7 preventable acute hospitalizations/yr (+£299.23M/yr INMB, b990361d) and 18,741.7 10-year ESKD dialysis patient-years (+£2,827.23M INMB, 91.5% DCEA acceptability, 51ae0545) unaddressed—shadow commissioning deployment is acceptably safe within its Non-SaMD boundary, pending local CSO DCB0160 sign-off.

Clinical Risk Management Plan (CRMP), NHS 5×5 Matrix & Decommissioning (1.7, 1.8, 3.3, 5.6)

  • NHS 5×5 Risk Matrix (3.3): Severity 1 Negligible .. 5 Catastrophic × Likelihood 1 Very Low (<0.1% code lock) .. 5 Very High (>25%) → Acceptable (1–3), Tolerable (4–6), Undesirable (8–12; requires §5.3 risk/benefit justification + DCB0160 SOP), Unacceptable (15–25; release blocked).
  • Automated CI Release Gates (4.6–4.8): Every build executes ocq safety-case, ocq sync-claims, ocq repro --tier tier0-offline (15/15 lanes), ocq check --online, and pytest.
  • Mandatory Review Triggers (RT-01..RT-06, 5.6): (1) NICE DG59 / NG203 / PMG36 or MHRA Drug Safety alerts; (2) any proposal to ingest NHS SDE pseudonymised patient feeds or connect to live EHRs (UKCA SaMD gate); (3) rolling 90-day ACI coverage < 85% or PK/CKM validation fold-error > 2.0x; (4) Gate G4 threshold or HUGR compiler changes; (5) ≥ 30% PCN broker turnover; (6) 12-monthly scheduled review.
  • Safe Decommissioning Protocol (H-OCQ-07, 1.8): 30-day CSO step-down notice (TC-OCQ-07), offline static HTML/PDF/ML-DSA-65 8-year audit archive export, FORK_PROTOCOL co-steward summary, and confirmation of standalone PCN BNF/KDIGO & Core20 checklist continuity.
